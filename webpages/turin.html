Turin

Turin, a city located in the northwestern part of Italy, boasts a rich and storied history that dates back to its origins as a Roman settlement known as 'Augusta Taurinorum,' established around 27 BC. This foundational period marked the city's importance as a strategic military and commercial center within the Roman Empire. The grid-like layout of its streets bears testimony to Roman urban planning, effectively serving transit and trade purposes in the region.

Cultural Experiences

Elegant and earnest, Turin boasts a one-of-a-kind artistic and cultural heritage: the elegant aristocratic residences of times gone by, breathtaking Baroque architecture, bountiful museums and priceless monuments still stand tall today, creating a unique blend between past and present.

Personal Anecdotes

Turin is rich with royal secrets, historical anecdotes, and local legends tied to its centuries as the capital of the House of Savoy.
The Historic Bicerin - Since the 18th century, locals at spots like Caffè Al Bicerin have warmed up with a bicerin. This traditional layered drink combines espresso, hot chocolate, and whole milk or cream.
