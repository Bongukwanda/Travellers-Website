Landmarks

Travelers widely praise Madrid's landmarks for mixing deep royal history with lively street life. Visitors consider the Royal Palace of Madrid majestic, the Prado Museum world-class for art, and public squares like Plaza Mayor and Puerta del Sol essential for the city's vibrant social atmosphere.

Cultural Experiences

Madrid offers rich cultural experiences through its world-class Golden Art Triangle museums like the Museo del Prado, historic royal landmarks, and vibrant culinary traditions ranging from old-school tapas in La Latina to authentic evening flamenco performances.

Personal Anecdotes

The people in Madrid are incredibly open, warmhearted, and always ready for a good conversation.
You end up chatting with people you’ve just met minutes ago – and quickly form new friendships that way. Madrileños have a strong sense of community. It’s easy to fit in here, as people from all over the world live in this city, and this multiculturalism only adds to Madrid's richness.
Looking back I wish I had carried a digital camera with me but still the pictures aren’t so bad…
